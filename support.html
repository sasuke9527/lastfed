爸爸妈妈怎么共用一份记录？

两台 iPhone 登录同一个 iCloud 账号，并在「设置 → 你的名字 → iCloud」里打开 iCloud 云盘，记录就会自动同步。不需要注册账号。

家人相册怎么用？家人怎么加入？

发起的人（需要 Pro）：「照片」→「家人相册」→ 选要共享的标签 →「开始共享，邀请家人」→ 点「拷贝邀请」，发给家人。家人（免费）：装好「几点喂的」→ 拷贝收到的那条邀请 →「照片」→「加入家人的相册」→ 点「粘贴」。两边的 iPhone 都要登录 iCloud。

照片放在发起人自己的 iCloud 里，只有收到邀请的人看得到。发起人可以随时移除某个人、拿掉某张照片或停止共享；家人也可以随时退出。相册里有不合适的内容，或者遇到别的问题，请发邮件到上面的地址。

买了 Pro，换手机或重装后怎么恢复？

打开 App →「设置」→「恢复购买」。同一个家庭共享群组里的家人也会自动解锁。

Pro 是订阅吗？

不是。一次性购买，不会自动续费。

语音记录听不懂？

语音记录目前只识别中文，并且只用手机上的离线识别。如果提示不能离线识别，到「设置 → 通用 → 键盘」打开听写并添加中文。

小组件、Siri 没反应？

小组件和 Siri / 快捷指令是 Pro 功能。解锁后，在 App 的「设置 → 快捷入口」里有每一种入口的设置步骤。

锁屏没有显示母乳计时？

到「设置 → 几点喂的」打开「实时活动」。

How do both parents share one set of records?

Sign both iPhones in to the same iCloud account and make sure iCloud is turned on for the app in Settings → your name → iCloud. Records then sync automatically — no sign-up needed.

How does Family Album work? How does my family join?

To start one (needs Pro): Photos → Family Album → choose the tags to share → Start Sharing and Invite Family → Copy Invitation, then send it to your family. To join (free): install LastFed → copy the invitation you received → Photos → Join a Family Album → Paste. Both iPhones need to be signed in to iCloud.

The photos stay in the owner's own iCloud and only people with the invitation can see them. The owner can remove a person or a photo, or stop sharing, at any time; family members can leave at any time. To report inappropriate content in an album, or any other problem, email us at the address above.

I bought Pro. How do I get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Open the app → Settings → Restore Purchases. Family members in the same Family Sharing group unlock automatically.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. It's a one-time purchase and never renews.

Voice logging doesn't understand me

Voice logging currently recognises Chinese only and uses on-device recognition. If the app says on-device recognition isn't available, turn on Dictation and add Chinese in Settings → General → Keyboard.

Widgets or Siri don't work

Widgets and Siri / Shortcuts are Pro features. After unlocking, Settings → Quick entry in the app has set-up steps for each one.

The breastfeeding timer isn't on my Lock Screen

Turn on Live Activities in Settings → LastFed.
